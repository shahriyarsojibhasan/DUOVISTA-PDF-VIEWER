Privacy Policy for DuoVista PDF Studio

Last Updated: November 2026

Developer: Shahriyar Sojib Hasan (Support Developer)

1. Overview & Commitment

DuoVista PDF Studio is committed to total user privacy. We believe students and researchers should be able to read, annotate, and compare documents with complete peace of mind. DuoVista PDF Studio does NOT collect, transmit, sell, or monetize any of your personal data or document contents.

2. Data Processing & Document Privacy

  • 100% Client-Side Processing: All PDF document parsing, page rendering, annotation rendering, and audio playback take place entirely on your local device using Mozilla's open-source PDF.js engine.
  • No Remote Storage: Your documents never leave your computer. We do not maintain any servers, databases, or cloud processing endpoints.

3. Local Storage (On Your Machine)

DuoVista PDF uses your browser's local storage (localStorage and Chrome Extension storage API) solely for your own convenience to save:

  • Your chosen reading theme (Light, Dark, or Sepia).
  • Your last preferred split ratio and layout mode (Up-Down or Side-by-Side).
  • Your answers in the IELTS Answer Sheet and scratchpad notes so you don't lose work when refreshing.

This data stays on your machine and can be cleared anytime by clicking "Clear All" or clearing browser data.

4. Extension Permissions Disclosure

  • storage: Used strictly to save local settings and answer sheet notes on your device.
  • webNavigation: Used strictly to detect when you open a .pdf URL in Chrome or Edge so it can display the PDF in DuoVista's split viewer instead of the browser's default reader.
  • tabs: Used to open viewer.html in a full browser tab when you click the extension toolbar icon.
  • contextMenus: Used to add a right-click option ("Open in DuoVista Split Viewer") on PDF links.

5. Third-Party Services & Telemetry

DuoVista PDF Studio contains zero analytics trackers, zero advertising SDKs, and zero telemetry.
